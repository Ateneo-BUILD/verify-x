eVerify fields

Every field a Tier II lookup returns, in one list. Display puts it on the verification document; Log records it in the transaction log, and switching Log off means the value is not kept at all, so it cannot be exported later either. A field with both off is never asked for.

One order, shared: the document prints its rows in this order and the export takes its columns from the same order, skipping what is not logged. Drag a row or use the arrows.

The name is what the document prints and what the CSV column is called. The fourteen at the top came with names, and so did Email Address, Mobile Number and Residency Status; the rest show their eVerify key until you give them one.

Age is not returned by eVerify: it is worked out from the date of birth on the day of the verification, and logged as it was that day. Displayed, it also follows the name at the top of the document. It is never put in the signed credential or on the phone card.
