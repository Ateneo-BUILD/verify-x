Development capture store

This instance keeps what eVerify returned. A successful lookup is stored whole — every field, and the face — so the printed document’s sizing can be measured against real data rather than against a cartoon. A failed one is stored too, with what the upstream said and no identity in it at all. It exists only where both environment opt-ins are set, so it cannot come into being on production. Empty it when a study is finished — nothing ages it out on a timer.
