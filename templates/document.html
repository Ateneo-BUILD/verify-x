{# /.doc-body -- everything above the cut line ends here #} {% comment %} The cut-out card: the holder's phone card, printed on its side. It must stay outside .doc-body -- inside it, it would be clipped along with everything else the moment the certificate ran long. This page draws no card of its own. `static/cardcanvas.js` renders the payload the server returned -- the same renderer, the same payload and the same design the holder saves to Photos -- and drawCutCard() turns it ninety degrees so its long side lies across the sheet.
