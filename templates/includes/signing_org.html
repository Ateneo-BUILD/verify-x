{% comment %} An organization's Credential signing section (accounts/signing_status.py for_organization). A site admin sees it on the organization's Site page and may start its next certificate there -- generated at once by the Organization CA, or requested from the PKI and uploaded when it returns -- and discard a key that is not yet published. Where the signer's policy lets the site publish, the admin also publishes the key and then starts it signing once its phones have it. The organization's own admins see the same facts on their Organization page, read-only. Who may press Publish and Start is `may_switch_keys`: a superuser's own login, or any site admin where the signer's policy sets `site_controls_all_keys`. Arguments: `org_signing`, `org`, and `upload` (true for a site admin). {% endcomment %} {% include "includes/signing_style.html" %}
