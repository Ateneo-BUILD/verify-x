{% comment %} Setup's Credential signing card, for site admins (verify/views/core.py setup; the rows come from accounts/signing_status.py). Its own card, below Setup's form, because its buttons are forms of their own. It may do three reversible things here: pause credential signing, suspend a key, and upload a certificate for a key the signer generated. An organization's own key is made, requested or discarded from its Site page. Everything else -- resume, publish, activate, unsuspend, withdraw, and the Organization CA itself -- needs someone on the signer host, so a row names the command instead of offering a button. Except where the signer's policy sets `site_controls_all_keys` (`signing.site_controls_all_keys` is its own word): then the deployment's keys are published and started here, and any key unsuspended or withdrawn, and signing resumed, by any site admin. {% endcomment %} {% include "includes/signing_style.html" %}
